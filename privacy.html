Pocket Decks privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026

What Pocket Decks is

Pocket Decks is a personal DJ mixer app made by Andrei Baloniu. It runs on your phone. There is no Pocket Decks server or account.

Google and YouTube data

If you sign in with Google, the app asks only for read-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly). It uses that access to show your playlists and liked videos and to search YouTube. It never changes anything in your account.

The sign-in is stored only on your phone, inside the app. It is never sent anywhere except to Google, to keep you signed in. Tap Sign out in the app to delete it, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Pocket Decks' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your music and mixes

Song files you load, remixes and recordings stay on your phone. They are never uploaded.

YouTube

YouTube tracks play through YouTube's own player, under the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
